FUTURE PHASE / OPTIONAL INTEGRATION

Mobile Room & Elevator Access Concept

Illustrates a possible future credential experience after vendor, engineering, emergency-mode and management review.

1. Active stay
Credential becomes available for a confirmed stay.
2. Allowed areas
Room and assigned floor logic; optional approved zones.
3. Loss / compromise
Credential can be revoked according to the approved process.
4. Checkout
Credential expires; physical-card fallback remains available.
Concept only. No A Z Hotel elevator, door lock, card system, booking system, NFC, Bluetooth, API or security log is connected or assumed. Fire-service operation and emergency override must be validated by the authorized vendors and qualified professionals before any deployment.
